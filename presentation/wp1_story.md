> **Core Takeaway:** WP1 converts `1,000 × 12` hospital waveforms into one leak-free, shared `84-D` dual-view dataset, uncovers a **`2.7×` heart-attack (`MI`) bias** inside automated hospital reports, and proves via blind $k$-Means that **sensor artifacts distort Euclidean distances (`−5.1 pp` purity)** while heavy disease overlap caps clustering at `~58%`—predicting WP3's limits and motivating WP4–WP5.

### Slide 1 — ECG Basics & Turning `1,000 × 12` Waves into Shared `84-D` Dual Views
**Visual:** [`figures/wp1_fig1_ecg_primer_and_artifacts.png`](./figures/wp1_fig1_ecg_primer_and_artifacts.png)

* **How to read a heartbeat (10s @ 100 Hz, `1,000 × 12` matrix):**
  **P-wave** (atrial contraction) → **QRS / R-peak** (main ventricular spike) → **T-wave** (electrical reset). **Lead II** aligns with the heart's natural electrical axis, giving the tallest, cleanest R-peaks for beat detection and **HRV** (mean RR, std RR, RMSSD).
* **Real hardware artifacts flagged in metadata (`Panel B`):**
  `baseline_drift` (`0.5–4 Hz` breathing/movement sway) warps amplitudes across leads; `static_noise` (`15–40 Hz` loose-lead buzz) inflates high-frequency variance.
* **Why extract one shared `84-D` dual-view table (`features.npz`) instead of raw `1,000 × 12` waves?**
  1. Classical ML (SVM, LogReg, $k$-NN, EM) cannot handle unaligned time offsets or `12,000` dimensions when Gold labels drop to $\eta=2\%$ (`180` records).
  2. One frozen `84-D` matrix guarantees an **apples-to-apples comparison** across WP3, WP4, and WP5—splitting naturally into **View 1 (`0:42`: Time, HRV, Morphology, Hjorth)** and **View 2 (`42:84`: Welch PSD bands + `db4` Wavelets)** required for WP4 Co-Training.

### Slide 2 — Patient-Stratified Splits & The `2.7× MI` Machine-Label Bias
**Visual:** [`figures/wp1_fig2_dataset_funnel_and_shift.png`](./figures/wp1_fig2_dataset_funnel_and_shift.png)

* **5 Superclasses (`21,837 → 16,272` single-label ECGs):**
  `NORM` (`~55%`), `MI` (`~15%`), `STTC` (`~15%`), `CD` (`~11%`), `HYP` (`~3%`). Patient-level 10-fold stratification prevents patient leakage across **Folds 1–8 (`12,978` train pool)**, **Fold 9 (`1,642` val)**, and **Fold 10 (`1,652` held-out test)**.
* **Gold vs. Untrusted inside Folds 1–8 (`validated_by_human`):**
  * **Gold Set $\mathcal{D}_L$ (`9,010` records, ~69%/fold):** Cardiologist-verified truth anchors (we subsample $\eta \in \{2,5,10,20,50\}\%$ in WP3–WP5).
  * **Untrusted Pool $\mathcal{D}_U$ (`3,968` records, ~31%/fold):** Autogenerated software labels $\tilde{y}$.
* **Crucial Motivation for WP2 & WP5:**
  `MI` is **`10.4%` in Gold vs. `28.0%` in Untrusted (`2.7×` spike)** while `NORM` drops `62.5% → 40.9%`. Machine labels carry **structured diagnostic bias**, and class imbalance forces **Macro F1** as our primary metric throughout WP2–WP5.

### Slide 3 — [Q1] Sensor Noise vs. Metric Geometry (Why Naive Euclidean Breaks)
**Visual:** [`figures/wp1_fig4_noise_geometry_redesign.png`](./figures/wp1_fig4_noise_geometry_redesign.png)

* **Why run blind $k$-Means ($k=5$) before building SSL classifiers?**
  Graph SSL (WP3) relies on the **Cluster Assumption** (*nearby ECGs share labels*). Clustering without labels directly tests **Q1**: whether `baseline_drift`/`static_noise` breaks Euclidean distance and how PCA / Mahalanobis respond.
* **Three clear findings (`Panel A` & `Panel B`):**
  1. **Artifacts degrade every metric:** **Raw + Euclidean** suffers the worst hit (`−5.1 pp` purity: `0.591 → 0.540`) because multi-lead drift dominates straight-line distance.
  2. **PCA cushions noise & sharpens clusters:** `PCA-20` trims the noise drop (`−4.2 pp`) and lifts Euclidean ARI by **+49%** (`0.0486 → 0.0723`).
  3. **Mahalanobis wins on full-pool structure:** Whitening correlated features via $\hat{\Sigma}^{-1}$ on all `12,978` training ECGs **more than doubles ARI (`0.0486 → 0.1081`, 2.2×)**.

### Slide 4 — PCA Compression, Disease Overlap & Foreshadowing WP3–WP5
**Visual:** [`figures/wp1_fig5_pca_and_overlap.png`](./figures/wp1_fig5_pca_and_overlap.png)

* **Effective dimensionality (`Panel A`):**
  On standardized training features, `5` PCs hold `52.0%` variance, `10` hold `70.4%`, **`20` hold `86.3%`**, and `24` cross `90%`.
* **Structural Ceiling & Direct Link to WP3–WP5 (`Panel B`):**
  In `(PC1, PC2)`, all 5 class medians sit packed together (`MI` and `STTC` overlap `NORM`), capping unsupervised purity at **`~0.58`**. This predicts two downstream results before any classifier runs:
  1. Pure label diffusion (WP3) will struggle across overlapping boundaries.
  2. Unlike WP1's stable $N=12,978$ global covariance, estimating Mahalanobis $\hat{\Sigma}_L$ on tiny Gold slices ($\eta=2\text{–}10\%$ in WP3) will require heavy PCA compression (`PCA=5`).

## Defense Notes (If Asked in Q&A)
* **"Why mention zero-padded View 1 dimensions?"** View 1 has 34 active physiological features + 8 zero-variance slots aligned to View 2's 42 dims; `StandardScaler`, PCA, L2-LogReg, RBF-SVM, and trees mathematically ignore zero-variance columns (zero distance/split contribution). *(Optional schematic backup: [`wp1_fig3_feature_architecture.png`](./figures/wp1_fig3_feature_architecture.png))*
* **"Why did WP1 favor Mahalanobis while WP3 rejected it at 84-D?"** Sample-size regime: WP1 fit $\hat{\Sigma}$ on `12,978` records ($N \gg d$); WP3's Eq. 4.1 fits $\hat{\Sigma}_L$ on `180–900` Gold records, inflating $84 \times 84$ matrix-inversion noise unless compressed to `PCA=5`.
